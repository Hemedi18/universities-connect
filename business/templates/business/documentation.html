U-Connect Documentation

Welcome to the comprehensive user guide for U-Connect. This documentation is designed to help you navigate every feature of the platform, from setting up your account to managing a student business.

U-Connect is a campus-centric marketplace built to facilitate safe and easy trading between students. Whether you are looking for textbooks, selling old electronics, or promoting your small business, this guide covers everything you need to know.

1. User Roles

Personal Seller

Students who want to sell used items (books, phones) occasionally. No business registration required.

Company Owner

Local businesses or student entrepreneurs with a brand name, logo, and regular stock (e.g., Food, Fashion).

Administrator

System managers who oversee users, verify companies, and manage the database via the Admin Panel.

3. Getting Started

Before you can start trading, you need to set up your presence on U-Connect. Follow these steps to get up and running.

Account Registration

To ensure safety and trust, most features require an account.

  1. Click on the Register link in the top navigation bar.
  2. Fill in your username, email address, and a strong password.
  3. Once registered, you will be automatically logged in.

Installing the App (PWA)

U-Connect is a Progressive Web App, meaning you can install it on your phone without visiting an app store.

  • On Android: Open Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), and select "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
  • On iOS: Open Safari, tap the Share button, and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • On Desktop: Look for the install icon in the browser address bar.

4. Buying Guide

Finding the right item on campus has never been easier. Here is a deep dive into finding and purchasing items.

Browsing and Searching
  • Categories: Use the "Buy" page to explore specific categories like Electronics, Books, or Fashion.
  • Search Bar: Enter keywords in the search bar at the top of the home page.
  • Filters: Use the sort dropdown to organize results by Price (Low to High), Newest First, or Popularity.
Understanding Item Details
  • Condition: Check if the item is New, Used, or Refurbished.
  • Location: See where on campus the item is located (e.g., Hall 4).
  • Seller Info: View the seller's profile to see if they are a verified student or a business.

5. Selling Guide

Turn your unused items into cash or run your student business efficiently.

Tip: Use good lighting and a plain background for your photos. Be honest about any defects or scratches.
How to Post an Item
  1. Click the Sell Item button in the navigation bar.
  2. Select Category: Choose the most relevant category (e.g., Electronics).
  3. Upload Photos: You can upload up to 3 images.
  4. Description: Be detailed. Mention the brand, model, age, and reason for selling.
  5. Price: Set a competitive price.
Business Tools (For Companies)

For student entrepreneurs and power sellers, the Company Dashboard offers advanced tools:

  • Analytics: See which types of products are getting the most views.
  • Bulk Listing: Manage high stock quantities efficiently.
  • Brand Visibility: Your shop appears on the dedicated "Companies" page.

6. Communication System

U-Connect features a built-in real-time chat system to keep your personal phone number private.

Instant Messaging

Chat happens in real-time without page reloads.

Image Sharing

Send additional photos of items directly in the chat.

Notifications

Receive alerts for new messages and order updates.

7. Admin Features

Administrators have full control over the platform via the Admin Panel.

  • User Management: Block abusive users or reset passwords.
  • Content Moderation: Delete fake items or inappropriate pictures.
  • Location Management: Add new Regions and Districts for company registration.
  • Category Management: Create new product categories.

8. FAQ & Troubleshooting

Ensure your image is in JPG or PNG format and under 5MB. If the issue persists, try refreshing the page or checking your internet connection.

Check the seller's profile to see their "Last Active" status. If they haven't been online recently, try finding a similar item from another seller. We encourage all users to reply within 24 hours.

Currently, account deletion must be handled by an administrator. Please contact support@u-connect.com with your request.
