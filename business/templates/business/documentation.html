{% trans "Register an Account" %}
  1. {% trans "Click the Register button in the top or bottom menu." %}
  2. {% trans "Fill in your username, university or personal email, phone number, and a secure password." %}
  3. {% trans "Enter the 6-digit OTP code sent to your email to verify your identity." %}
  4. {% trans "You can immediately start browsing, buying, and listing items!" %}

  • iOS (iPhone): {% trans "Open Safari, tap Share and select 'Add to Home Screen'." %}

{% trans "5. Selling Guide" %}

{% trans "Tip:" %} {% trans "Take clear, well-lit photos and write an honest description of your product's condition to sell faster!" %}
{% trans "Steps to Upload a Product" %}
  1. {% trans "Click the Sell button in the menu." %}
  2. {% trans "Select the correct Category for your product (e.g. Phones & Tablets)." %}
  3. {% trans "Upload up to 3 clean photos showing front and back views." %}
  4. {% trans "Fill in technical specifications with accurate metrics (such as mAh for battery, GB for RAM and storage, Megapixels for camera)." %}
  5. {% trans "Preview your product card on the right, then click Publish Listing." %}
